# Community Standards

We want a professional, accessible engineering-learning community.

- Treat beginners respectfully and give actionable code-review feedback.
- Critique ideas and implementation evidence, not the person's identity or background.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, spam or expose private information about others.
- Ask for one focused hint rather than reposting someone else's solution without permission.
- Respect intellectual property and attribution.
- Do not request or publish credentials or customer data.

Maintainers may moderate abusive content or restrict accounts participating in the repository. Use GitHub report or block controls for abuse. For security-sensitive concerns, consult [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
